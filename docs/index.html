Your voice, handled with care.

Your recording is turned into text by Groq’s speech model and then cleaned up. This app doesn’t keep your recordings. Nothing is saved on your phone either, except your chosen language.

What early users say

Good questions.

Why was the first try slow?

The free server goes to sleep when nobody uses it. The first request wakes it up, which can take up to a minute. After that it’s fast.

Can it type into other apps?

Not yet. For now you copy the text and paste it. An Android keyboard is planned so it can type anywhere.

Which languages work today?

You can speak Hindi, English or a mix, and choose Hinglish, English or Hindi for the result.

Why is a word wrong sometimes?

This is an early version. Names and unusual words can slip through. Speaking close to the mic in a quiet place helps.
